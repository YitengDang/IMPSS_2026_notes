**11:30—11:55    748, From 3D imaging to quantitative signatures: AI-driven digitalization of microphysiological systems**
Anne Beghin, National University of Singapore, Singapore

Very relevant talk as it discussed 3D imaging of organoids and the image computational challenges associated with it. 

MPS/organoids images are much harder to analyze than conventional 3D models
3D AI-based analysis without user training needed
DeepStart3D: nuclear segmentation (based on StarDist)
Organosegmenter software ([Nature Methods 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02685-4))
	OrganoProfiler
-> Spatial phenotyping of organoids at single cell resolution

Applications:
	1. Organoids in space (EM)
	2. Secretome analysis (EVs with EM)

Microdome chips for mass production
-> Multi-platform, worldwide, high-content screening

**11:55—12:15    687, Measuring learning and memory in human brain organoids using organoid intelligence and Pac-Man game**  
Corey Rountree, 28bio Inc., Metairie, LA, USA

[Startup company](https://www.28bio.com/) with a cool vision of developing "Organoid intelligence"
Using human brain organoids to model memory and learning
Train organoids to more closely resemble human spike activity
After many rounds of training, organoids were able to play Pac-Man quite well
