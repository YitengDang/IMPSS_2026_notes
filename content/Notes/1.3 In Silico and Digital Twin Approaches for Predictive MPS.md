**2:00—2:25    819, A precision medicine platform: integrated patient digital and biomimetic twins for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD)**
Eric Strobl, University of Pittsburgh, Pittsburgh, PA, USA

Patient heterogeneity: who benefits?
Outcome heterogeneity: which benefits?
Clinomics: collect a set of measurements with consistent time pattern

Three types of digital twins:
	• Mechanistic / simulation
	• ML / forecasting
	• Covariate-adjusted treatment models

Outcome learning (look up papers)
	• SCORE
	• DEBIAS
	
**2:25—2:45    334, Digital twin–integrated microphysiological systems for predicting subcutaneous drug transport**  
Seunggyu Kim, Korea University, Sejong, Sejong, Republic of Korea  
Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge, MA, USA  

Former postdoc from the lab of [Roger Kamm](https://meche.mit.edu/people/faculty/rdkamm@mit.edu).

The computational approach here focussed on spatial simulations of chemical species and hydrodynamics.
* MPS -> digital twin -> AI surrogate model -> PK parameters
* Membrane transport: Kedem-Katchalsky equations

**2:45—3:05    536, Development of a digital twin for the investigation of opioid overdose treatment utilizing the human-on-a-chiptm multi-organ microphysiological system**  
Aakash Patel, Hesperos, Orlando, FL, USA  

Another talk where the "AI" component is basically PK/PD modelling. 
* Digital twin for opioid overdose
* Multi-organ system with multiple readouts
* Use MPS model data to develop a PK/PD model
[Hesperos](https://hesperosinc.com/) is one of the older OoC companies and was founded by one of the key figures behind IMPSS, so they got lots of publicity at the conference.
